Centre Information

Lochquarry Outdoor Centre is set in acres of land in the heart of the majestic Argyll hills. On our doorstep is not only magnificent scenery, but also a breath-taking selection of outdoor and adventurous activities. With activities designed to meet the needs of all ages and experiences of young people aged 6-18, Lochquarry truly brings adventure to everyone.

We have recently expanded the number of different activities that we offer and are thrilled about all the exciting activities that young people can take part in at Lochquarry.
