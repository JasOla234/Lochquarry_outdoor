Welcome to Lochquarry

The ultimate outdoor playground where adventure meets the wild. Spread across our spectacular natural landscape, our purpose-built activity zones are designed to challenge your limits, test your skills, and immerse you in nature.

Explore our three dedicated adventure zones:
The Loch & Waterfront

Dive into action on our expansive, pristine waters. Whether you're looking for a peaceful, rhythmic paddle along the shoreline or a high-speed, adrenaline-pumping wake, our water arena offers the perfect escape for every pace. Water based activities in the zone includes; Kayaking, Canoeing and Powerboating.

Mountain Zone & Crags

Reach new heights on our towering climbing faces and high-altitude elements. Engineered for both beginners and seasoned adrenaline-seekers, this zone is where you test your grip, find your balance, and enjoy the best views on the estate. Rope based activities in the zone includes; Climbing, Abseiling, Pole climbing.

The Woodlands & Ranges

Step into the shelter of the trees to hone your focus and precision. Our dedicated, safe range and forest areas are designed for those who want to slow things down, find their mark, and master ancient skills of accuracy. Land based activities in this zone includes; Hillwalking, Archery, Orienteering, Axe throwing.
